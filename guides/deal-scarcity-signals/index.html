Scarcity signals that mean a deal won’t last

The best cashflowing listings don’t sit. These are the signals we surface on Opportunity Radar.

Days on market vs yield

High yield + short DOM usually means competition. Low yield + long DOM is often a pricing or condition problem, not a bargain.

Competing offers / broker heat

When brokers say “multiple interested parties,” treat it as a process signal — not proof of value. Still run your own ROI.

Yield leaderboard pressure

If a deal sits near the top of a public cashflow rank, assume others see it too. Vanish risk is the point of Pro alerts.

What isn’t scarcity

Pretty photos, “motivated seller” fluff, or a huge ask with no NOI. Scarcity without cashflow is just hype.
