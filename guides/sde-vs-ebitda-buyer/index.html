SDE vs EBITDA — which number should a buyer trust?

Wrong metric = wrong multiple. Here’s the short buyer version.

SDE (seller’s discretionary earnings)

Best for owner-operated small businesses where the seller’s salary and perks are mixed into the P&L. SDE answers: “What can this throw off for one full-time owner-operator?”

EBITDA

Better when management is already in place or you’re buying a larger company / multi-unit with professional ops. EBITDA answers: “What’s operating profit before capital structure?”

Rule of thumb

  • Mom-and-pop / single location → start with SDE
  • Multi-site / manager-run → lean EBITDA
  • CRE (NNN / MF) → NOI and cap rate matter more than either
